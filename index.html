Zero-knowledge architecture

We can't read your data. That's the point.

Most "secure" apps encrypt data on a server they control — meaning they technically can read it. Hecate takes a different approach: encryption happens entirely on your device, before anything is written to disk or synced.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption on-device, not on a server
  • Master key stored in the iOS Keychain, bound to your device
  • iCloud sync sends only encrypted bytes — never plaintext
  • No password reset, no data recovery, no back door
  • Developer collects zero data from the app
